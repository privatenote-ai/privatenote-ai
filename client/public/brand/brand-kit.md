# PrivateNote.ai Brand Kit

## Core Identity

- Product name: `PrivateNote.ai`
- Wordmark style in UI: `privateNote.ai`
- Primary color: `#0000FF`
- Primary background: `#F8FBFF`
- Paper/letter tones: `#FFFDF8`, `#EDE6D8`
- Text: `#0F172A`
- Muted text: `#475569`
- Borders: `#D7E2F0`
- Font: Inter

## Logo

- Mark: `privateNote-logo-mark.svg`
- Lockup: `privateNote-logo-lockup.svg`
- Keep the logo on white, near-white, or primary-blue backgrounds.
- Do not add gradients to the mark. The current brand signal is the solid blue tile plus cream envelope.

## Social Templates

- Instagram square: `instagram-post-template.svg` at `1080x1080`
- Instagram story: `instagram-story-template.svg` at `1080x1920`
- Facebook / Open Graph post: `facebook-post-template.svg` at `1200x630`
- Facebook cover: `facebook-cover-template.svg` at `1640x924`

## Voice

- Plain, direct, privacy-first.
- Avoid fear-heavy copy.
- Prefer concrete claims: encrypted in browser, read-once, no plaintext on server, no tracking.

## Reusable Copy

- `Share secrets. Leave no trail.`
- `Private by design.`
- `Encrypted notes that disappear after reading.`
- `No accounts. No tracking. No plaintext on the server.`
- `Use privacy as a workflow, not a panic button.`
